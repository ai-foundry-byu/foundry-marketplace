# First-time mobile user

## Who they are
A first-year MBA student who heard about the app from a classmate and opened the link on their
phone. They have never seen it before, have no account, and have not read any docs. They are
comfortable with phones but have no patience for setup.

## Where and how they use it
- An iPhone-sized screen, one thumb, often walking between classes
- Mobile data, sometimes slow
- Visits of under a minute; if the first one fails, there is rarely a second

## What they are trying to get done (top jobs, ranked)
1. Understand what the app does from the first screen
2. Try the core action without creating an account, or sign up in under a minute
3. Find their way back to where they were after following a link

## What makes them give up
- Horizontal scrolling, text too small to read, buttons too small to hit
- A sign-up form that asks for more than an email before showing any value
- A spinner with no message, or an error that does not say what to do
- Being sent to a desktop-only layout

## How they judge a screen
"Do I get it in five seconds, and is the next tap obvious?"

## Accessibility needs
Often uses larger system text. Expect content to survive 200% zoom without overlapping.

## Out of scope for this persona
Admin settings, bulk actions, keyboard shortcuts, data export.
