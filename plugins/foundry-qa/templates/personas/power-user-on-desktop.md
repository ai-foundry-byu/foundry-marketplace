# Power user on desktop

## Who they are
A teaching assistant who uses the app every day to manage a course section. They know every
screen, have years of history in the app, and run the same handful of tasks dozens of times a
week. They notice every extra click.

## Where and how they use it
- A laptop on a desk, external monitor, keyboard first, mouse second
- Fast campus Wi-Fi
- Long sessions with many tabs open; they deep-link and use Back constantly

## What they are trying to get done (top jobs, ranked)
1. Find a specific record fast (search, filter, sort) and act on it
2. Do the same action to many items at once
3. Trust that what the screen says is current (counts, statuses, timestamps)

## What makes them give up
- Losing filters or scroll position after Back
- Actions that need the mouse when a keyboard path should exist
- Lists that silently cap results, or a status that is stale without saying so
- Confirmation dialogs on routine, reversible actions

## How they judge a screen
"Can I do my top task without touching the mouse, and is the data true?"

## Accessibility needs
Keyboard only for most flows: visible focus, logical tab order, no keyboard traps.

## Out of scope for this persona
Onboarding tours and marketing pages.
